Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 23, 2026

Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how data is managed within the Visual Risk & AI Intelligence desktop management dashboard environment.

1. Zero Remote Tracking Pledge

This system architecture functions strictly locally on your environment engine loop. No cookies, session identifiers, tracking beacons, or digital fingerprints are transmitted to global platforms, remote domains, or web servers.

2. Data Collection & Injection Matrix

When you use the data forms to inject entries (such as task durations, focus categories, and transaction amounts), that parameters data is stored temporarily in your local web container cache. We do not gather, share, sell, or process your personal log variables.

3. Third-Party CDN Resource Libraries

The application templates fetch specific external styling layers and visualization modules from trusted CDNs (including Font Awesome Icons and Chart.js). These requests only retrieve open-source code libraries to power the charts on your client interface.

4. User Rights & Data Cleansing

Because your structural tracking metrics reside locally in the browser runtime state memory, you hold absolute authority over your analytics logs. You can wipe all history entries instantly by cleaning your browser site storage cache or killing the local server thread pipeline.
